# EUROPAVERSE – XR Hackathon Submission

EUROPAVERSE is an immersive XR experience built in Unity for the Meta Quest 3, blending Virtual Reality (VR) and Mixed Reality (MR) into a seamless interactive journey.

---

## Platform
- Device: Meta Quest 3  
- Build Type: Android APK (Standalone)  
- Engine: Unity (XR)

---

## Repository Structure
/EuropaVerseUnity/ # Full Unity project files

/ProjectSubmission/QR_Codes/ # QR codes for MR interactions (REQUIRED!!!!)

---

## Getting Started

1. Install the provided APK on your Meta Quest 3.
2. Launch the application inside the headset.
3. The experience begins immediately in VR.

---

## Experience Flow

### Scene 1 – Lobby (VR)
- Look around to explore the environment.
- Locate the "Let’s Go" button.
- Aim at it using your controller (ray pointer will appear).
- Press the trigger to continue.

This transitions to the next scene.

---

### Scene 2 – Europa-Park (VR)
- Enter a themed VR environment.
- Find Euromaus (waving character).
- Move toward the guided marker.
- Receive instructions upon approaching.

#### Portal Interaction
- A portal appears after the interaction.
- Move into the portal to continue.

This transitions into Mixed Reality.

---

### Scene 3 – Mixed Reality (MR)
- The experience switches to MR.
- Complete boundary setup if prompted.

---

## QR Code Interaction

To trigger MR events:

1. Open one of the provided QR codes on a phone or screen:
   - Zombie Event
   - Firework Event  
2. While wearing the headset, look at the QR code.

No controller input is required.

---

## Events
Scanning a QR code triggers:
- Zombie interactions  
- Firework visual effects  

---

## Notes
- Ensure QR codes are well-lit and clearly visible.
- Maintain a reasonable distance for detection.
- No additional input is required after scanning.

---

## Development Notes
